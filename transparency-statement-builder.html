Transparency Statement Builder

Turn “I used ChatGPT” into a disclosure that tells a reader what AI actually contributed, what remained human, and what was verified.

Use the builder for student work, research notes, creative projects, or instructor-created materials. Adapt the final wording to local policy.

1. What kind of work is this?

2. What did AI help with?

3. What did you verify?

4. What remained your responsibility?

Your transparency statement

Core principle

Disclosure should describe contribution, not merely name the tool.

A useful statement helps a reader understand what AI did, what was checked, and where human judgment remained.

Weak statement

“I used ChatGPT for this assignment.”

Stronger statement

“I used ChatGPT to brainstorm possible subtopics and generate alternative search terms. I located and evaluated all sources independently and wrote the final analysis myself.”
