Three investigations

Practice decisions, not only rules.

Each case asks learners to inspect evidence, choose an interpretation, receive explanatory feedback, and recover from mistakes. A complete case generally takes 15 to 30 minutes.

Case 01

The Rewritten Page

Responsible AI use, disclosure, assignment rules, academic integrity, and distributed responsibility.

Case 02

The Citation That Never Existed

Fabricated sources, mismatched identifiers, misquotation, relevance, and independent verification.

Case 03

The Borrowed Voice

Consent, representation, attribution, hidden labor, bias, power, and proportionate institutional responses.

Designed for teaching and adaptation

  • No ChatGPT or commercial AI account required
  • Browser-based progress without an account
  • Optional hints and supported answers without score penalties
  • Keyboard controls, skip links, visible focus, high contrast, and text alternatives
  • Source code, instructor guidance, customization notes, and accessibility documentation

Adaptations and LMS embeds should receive fresh accessibility, privacy, and server testing.
