Overview

What the framework is trying to do

Veldhuis and colleagues adapt a long-standing critical literacy tradition to AI. The framework uses four dimensions: disrupting the commonplace, considering multiple viewpoints, focusing on the sociopolitical, and taking action.

The model is significant because it does not stop at recognizing bias or harm. It asks learners to interrogate normalized assumptions, examine power and representation, and consider action or transformation.

Framework structure

Core dimensions or competencies

Disrupting the commonplace

Questioning what is normalized or taken for granted about AI.

Considering multiple viewpoints

Examining whose experiences and perspectives are represented or marginalized.

Focusing on the sociopolitical

Connecting AI to power, institutions, culture, politics, surveillance, and inequality.

Taking action

Moving from critique toward design, participation, civic response, or change.

Critical depth

Why this framework is rated Very High

Rated Very High because social power, representation, democratic participation, surveillance, accountability, ecological effects, and action are central to the framework rather than supplemental ethical topics.

Evidence base

What supports the framework?

Scoping/systematic synthesis of empirical critical AI literacy activities

Practical use

Especially useful for

Critical pedagogy, curriculum analysis, qualitative coding, research design, and development of socially oriented AI literacy interventions.

Evaluation

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Strong grounding in critical literacy theory.
  • Moves beyond ethical awareness toward action.
  • Makes sociopolitical and representational questions explicit.

Limitations

  • Most underlying interventions involve children and adolescents rather than higher education learners.
  • Verification of generated claims is less central than social critique.

Portability

How well does it travel?

Moderate–High. The four critical literacy dimensions transfer well, but higher education implementations should adapt examples and developmental expectations.
