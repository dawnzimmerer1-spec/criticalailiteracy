Assignment Retrofit Checklist

Redesign an existing assignment without throwing it into the pedagogical wood chipper. Keep the learning goal, then make process, evidence, judgment, and responsible AI choices more visible.

Retrofit progress

0 of 27 checks complete

1. Clarify the learning evidence

2. Decide where AI belongs

3. Make process visible

4. Strengthen evidence requirements

5. Build in critical evaluation

6. Protect agency and authorship

7. Address transparency

8. Check access and equity

9. Reconsider grading

Final retrofit test

After revising the assignment, ask:

If a student used generative AI appropriately, could I still see what that student knows, how they reasoned, what evidence they used, and what decisions they made?

If the answer is yes, the assignment is probably becoming more resilient. If the answer is no, add more visible process or judgment.

Retrofit, do not merely restrict.

The goal is not to make every assignment AI-proof. The goal is to make learning visible enough that appropriate AI use cannot erase the evidence you actually care about.

Start small

You do not need to rebuild the entire assignment. Pick two or three weak points, such as unclear AI rules, invisible process, or thin source requirements.

Fast faculty workshop

Give instructors one existing assignment and 20 minutes. Ask them to complete Sections 1, 2, 3, and 9 first.
